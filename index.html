What We Do

Plumbing Services in Sterling Heights MI

We are specialists in the repair and installation of almost all plumbing applications, both residential and commercial. If you don’t see what you need, just ask.

Drain Cleaning

Clogged drains, sinks, floor drains, and basement backups cleared fast. We use the most advanced equipment to unclog your drain without damaging your pipes. Hair, grease, soap scum — whatever the cause, we fix it right.

Pipe Leak Repair

A pipe leak or cracked pipe can cause serious water damage quickly. Our experienced plumbers can repair or replace any pipe — including frozen and burst pipes — and get your water service back online fast.

Water Heater Repair & Replacement

From minor repairs to full replacements, we service all makes and models of water heaters. We carry parts on our trucks so most repairs are completed in a single visit.

Toilet Repair & Replacement

Broken toilet, running toilet, clogged toilet — we handle it all. We also install new toilets from top brands like American Standard, Kohler, and Moen. Fast, clean, and done right the first time.

Faucet & Fixture Repair

Leaky faucets, shower and tub faucet repair, garbage disposal repair and replacement, instant hot installation, and more. We work with all major brands and provide an estimate before any work begins.

Emergency Plumbing

Plumbing emergencies don’t wait for business hours — and neither do we. We offer true 24-hour emergency service throughout Sterling Heights and Metro Detroit. Call us anytime, day or night.
